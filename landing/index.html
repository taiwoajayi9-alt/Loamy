Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to garden before?

No. Loamy is made for first-timers. Tell us about your space and we’ll suggest plants that suit it, then guide you step by step.

Do I need an account?

Not to explore. Browsing plants, checking “Can I grow this here?” and seeing your plan preview are free with no account. You’ll create a free account to save your plan, become a member or place a shop order.

How does delivery work?

We deliver within Lagos. You pay for your items at checkout, then we call you on the number you gave us to agree the delivery fee and a time that suits you.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes, any time from your profile. Your membership stops renewing, and you keep full access until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Is my payment safe?

Payments are handled by Paystack. Loamy never sees or stores your card details.
